## 2026-11-15 fill-in-the-blank question type (user screenshot request)
- New quiz kind `blank` （填空）, rendered in TimedQuiz after the screenshot's layout with this site's own code: same top bar （退出 capsule / 第 X/Y 题 / big countdown / thin progress bar / round sound toggle), heading 「填入括号里单词的正确形式。」, white stem card with teal left bar and red 「填空」 tag, the full sentence in large bold type with the target word as a light-teal highlighted block with teal underline (before checking it shows the bracketed base word, e.g. （solve）; after checking it shows the correct form), other words dotted-underlined (in-vocab words still tappable for lookup), a 「你的答案」 big input (correct → light-teal fill + teal border + ✓; wrong → red fill/border + shake + 正确答案 line), the shared white 解释 card with original Chinese explanations, and the centered deep-teal 「下一题 →」 capsule. Judging ignores case and surrounding whitespace; the usual rules hold (30s per question, timer keeps running after answering, timeout counts as wrong, exit records the interrupted attempt in history, where blank rows read 「你填对了/没填对」).
- Blank questions are derived, not separately authored: grammar blanks come from existing single-blank choice questions whose correct answer is a single word — the answer is placed back into the sentence and the base form is shown in brackets. Base-form derivation (irregular table + suffix rules) is validated against the site's own vocabulary plus a core word list; when the base cannot be verified (e.g. something, mother, teacher), the question stays multiple-choice rather than showing a guessed base. Vocab blanks are cloze questions built from word example sentences (the word or its form in the example is the answer; the dictionary word is the bracket hint) with original Chinese explanations referencing the example's translation.
- Mixing: grammar quizzes convert 3 （简单） / 6 （中等） / 9 （困难） of the picked questions to blanks in place (counts unchanged at 20/30/40); grammar placement converts 10 of 60; vocab practice interleaves up to 5 blanks among the choice questions (dictation unchanged at the end); topic placement turns up to 5 of its middle questions into blanks (copy updated to 「25 道练习与填空 + 5 道听写」).

## 2026-11-15 original reward system (user request: 四个独创奖励机制，学习内容不动)

Weekly-challenge limited badges appear in the same wall.
- XP 奖励商店 (profile, plus home entry card): server-priced catalog — 提示卡 30 XP, 免错卡 50 XP (consumables, stack), three seed-tree skins 120 XP and three avatar frames 80 XP (one-time). buyItem verifies balance/ownership server-side and deducts XP for real. Cards work inside TimedQuiz: hint removes one wrong option (choice questions, 1 per quiz), shield converts one wrong answer to correct for score/pass (1 per quiz), both decremented server-side via useCard. Equipped skin/frame is a local display preference (appearance.ts, localStorage, same pattern as display settings); ownership always comes from the server inventory. Skin recolors the tree panel gradient/progress bar; frame renders as a ring shadow on all avatars.
- 每周挑战 (home): one challenge per week, rotating deterministically by Monday-date hash among 本周完成 5 次练习 / 本周记住 50 个词 / 本周打卡 5 天， progress computed server-side from this week's real attempts (finished, non-placement), this week's newly-known word marks, and this week's study days. Completion claims +30 XP chest and lights that week's limited badge; unclaimed weeks simply roll over.
- 学习伙伴豆豆 (home, beside the seed tree): generated flat-style puppy illustration with a blue flower hair clip (media-generation pet-doudou asset, copied to client/src/assets/pet-doudou.webp). Five stages by total XP thresholds (0/100/300/700/1500: 幼犬/学步/勤奋/博学/学霸）, shown by growing size + stage face emoji + stage strip. Level-up plays a bounce+fade animation once (server-side seen_level via markPetSeen) and toasts. Feeding requires today's study check-in, once per day, persisted in pet_state; feeding plays the happy-bounce animation with a random encouragement line; tapping 豆豆 speaks a random encouragement line.
